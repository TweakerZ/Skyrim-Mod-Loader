Skyrim Mod Loader 0.1.4 moves the interface to the shared Material You design system and adds a new launcher icon.

**What's new**

- The app draws with the shared `skyrim-suite-ui` Material You design system, with dynamic colour from the wallpaper on Android 12+ and matching light and dark fallback schemes.
- New launcher icon, including background, foreground and monochrome layers for themed icons.
- The native window and system bars follow the system light and dark mode.
- Removed the obsolete Fullscreen button from the Mods card.

**Install**

Download `SkyrimModLoader-0.1.4.apk` and install it over the existing app. Android 11 (API 30) or newer.
